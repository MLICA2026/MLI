Grading

What to grade, how to grade on the platform, and the rubrics.

🧮 What to grade

You mainly handle EE (writing) and EO (oral). The written exam (CO · CE · EE) is on the platform: Course Homepage → Quiz (opens only at class time).

Mandatory — immigration record

Grades and transcripts are part of each student's official study record for immigration. A missing transcript means a passed exam can't be proven — grade every student on time.

SkillWhat you do
CO — ListeningMultiple-choice, graded automatically. Nothing to do.
CE — ReadingMultiple-choice, graded automatically. Nothing to do.
EE — WritingGrade manually on the platform (GRADE QUIZ): score + feedback.
EO — OralRecord your rubric score / feedback in the blank EO field (after the writing section).
Deadline

Finish grading within 3 working days.

🖥️ On the platform

The written exam sits in Quizzes (Course Homepage → Quiz, open only at class time). To grade a submission, click GRADE QUIZ, then open the student's Attempt to correct it. After you submit feedback, the student sees their score, the reference answers, and your comments on the writing and speaking parts.

📊 EE rubrics & NCLC

Two rubrics are used, depending on the exam. In both, you enter points in the yellow columns and the score is calculated for you.

RubricUsed forHow it scores
MLI rubricA1, A2, Phase 1, Phase 2 (mid)Content /8 · Grammar /7 · Spelling /5 · Word count /5 = /25
TCF rubricPhase 2 Final, CLB5, CLB6, CLB74 criteria × /5 (Instruction · Coherence · Vocabulary · Grammar & spelling) = /20 → auto NCLC

MLI rubric — criteria (A1, A2, Phase 1, Phase 2 mid)

CriterionPoints
1. Topic / content (letter format, describes new city & life)/8
2. Grammar (A1–A2 structures)/7
3. Spelling/5
4. Word count (≥50 words = 5; scales down to 0 under 10)/5
Total/25

TCF rubric — criteria (Phase 2 Final, CLB5, CLB6, CLB7)

Three tasks: 1 — short informal message (~60–120 words); 2 — formal message / letter / article (~120–150); 3 — synthesis of two documents + argued opinion (~120–180). The /20 covers the whole production.

CriterionPoints
1. Respect of the instruction & the three tasks/5
2. Coherence & cohesion/5
3. Vocabulary / lexis/5
4. Morphosyntax & spelling/5
Total/20 → NCLC
Give one direct score

The official TCF way tags each task with a CEFR level (A1 / A2 / B1 / B2). MLI needs a single number instead: on the TCF rubric, score the four criteria 0–5 for the production as a whole to get a mark out of 20 — the sheet converts it to NCLC automatically. Enter that score on the platform.

Revising a graded answer

If an answer was already graded but is incorrect, or there is a capitalization issue:

  1. Retract feedback.
  2. Click the answer states to correct them.

Answer-state colours: Green = correct · Red = wrong · Transparent background = unselected / default.
