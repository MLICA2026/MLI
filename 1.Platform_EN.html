Platform

Everything inside a course — pick a section.

📢 Announcements

Use this section for important notices, exam information, public holidays, and schedule changes.

Students also receive an email notification for each announcement.

✅ Attendance

Mandatory — immigration record

Immigration requires a study record for every student. If attendance is missing, a student's French result can't be documented, even after they pass. Update it on time. Classes with no attendance record also forfeit the attendance bonus.

This section records both student and teacher attendance.

  • The attendance page becomes active after the class start time.
  • Changes can be made within 24 hours.
  • Please update attendance promptly.

📝 Quizzes

All exams and quiz grading happen in this section.

Students can

  • Access the exam within the scheduled time
  • Click START QUIZ to open the exam page
  • Complete the quiz within the time limit
  • Submit answers before the deadline

Students are generally allowed two attempts, in case of unexpected system issues. If a student hits a technical problem, they should tell the teacher immediately.

Teachers can

  • Monitor exam attempts
  • Review submissions (GRADE QUIZ)
  • Manually grade subjective questions (GRADE QUIZ)
  • Provide feedback (GRADE QUIZ)

To grade a submission, open the student's attempt and click Attempt to correct the exam. After grading, students see their results, the reference answers, and teacher feedback for the writing and speaking parts (once you click Submit feedback).

Deadline

Complete grading within three working days — including reviewing submissions, manually grading subjective questions, and providing feedback.

📚 Homework

This section includes supplementary assignments, automatic grading for multiple-choice questions, and reference answers for written questions.

Teacher steps

  1. Click on each student's submission
  2. Select Grade homework
  3. Submit feedback

Students will then see their results, the correct answers, and teacher comments.

🎥 Recordings

Zoom recordings are automatically saved to the Recordings section if you joined every session.

  • Recordings are available for 7 days.
  • Students may use them for review and revision.

💬 Group Chat

Each course has a class discussion group (Discussions) where teachers and students can message each other.
