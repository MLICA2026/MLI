Teacher Handbook

In class

What is expected during every lesson: timing, breaks, what to say (and not say) in front of students.

⏰ On time

  • Be in the classroom (or Zoom room) 5 minutes before the scheduled start.
  • Teach the full scheduled time. A class cannot be shortened without authorisation.
  • Follow the course plan: do not skip lessons, cut content, or fall behind schedule.
  • If you finish the planned content early, use the remaining time for review, extra exercises or practice activities. Do not end class early.
Progress checks

Academic Affairs checks class progress every two weeks. If a class is under 80% of the planned stage, adjustments are agreed together with Academic Affairs.

☕ Breaks & timer

Each 3-hour class includes 15 minutes of break in total. You choose one of two formats:

OptionHow it works
A · One break15 minutes, once, around the middle of the class.
B · Two breaks10 minutes after the first hour, then 5 minutes after the second hour.
15 minutes is the maximum

Breaks must not run over. Teaching resumes as soon as the timer reaches zero, even if a few students are still away.

Use the countdown timer

The courseware includes a countdown timer. Put it on screen for every break so students know exactly when to come back.

  1. Tell students how long the break is (15, 10 or 5 minutes).
  2. Open the countdown timer from the materials and click the preset: 15 min, 10 min or 5 min.
  3. Share your screen on Zoom and click Démarrer (or press Space). Press F for full screen. Leave it shared for the whole break.
  4. When it reaches zero, stop sharing and restart the lesson straight away.
Tip

Announce the return time out loud and in the Zoom chat as well ("We start again at 7:40"), for students who step away before the timer is on screen.

🤝 In front of students

Contract obligation

Teachers are strictly forbidden from criticising, denigrating or challenging the teaching materials, manuals, curriculum, course structure or exam materials in front of students.

❌ Do not say✅ Say instead
"This material is bad.""Let's add an example to help everyone understand."
"You don't need to read this chapter.""Here is another way to say this. You can compare the two."
"I don't agree with how this course / exam is organised."(Nothing in class. Send your feedback to the Academic Department.)
Principle

Add to the material, never contradict it. If you spot an error or have a suggestion, tell the Academic Department after class. Your feedback is welcome there.

Also off-limits in class

  • Business topics: your pay, tuition fees, pricing, school operations or marketing. Politely direct students (or parents) to the school administration or the academic coordinator.
  • Any confidential information about MLI, its staff or its students.

📷 Camera & attendance

Cameras (online classes)

  • Keep your camera on for the whole class, except for approved exceptional circumstances or a technical failure.
  • Require students to keep their cameras on too. You control the microphone settings.
  • A student whose camera is off three times in a row is counted absent and must be reported to Academic Affairs.
Camera-off incidents (teacher)

Incidents are cumulative and do not reset each month. 1st–2nd: written reminder. 3rd: formal email, recorded as a teaching violation, and the perfect-attendance bonus is lost for that month. Academic Affairs runs random checks.

Attendance

  • Record presence, late arrivals and early departures accurately for every class on the MLI platform.
  • Students under 90% attendance must retake the course, so your record matters.

How to record it: see guide 1 · Platform → Attendance.

🔴 Recordings

Mandatory

Every class is recorded as a condition of teaching, for student review and quality assurance. Access is limited to enrolled students and designated MLI staff. Recordings are deleted within 7 days.

Recordings are used only for internal purposes: make-up for absent students and post-class review.

Not allowed

  • Recording classes on a personal device.
  • Saving or sharing recordings without authorisation.
  • Posting course content on external platforms.

How to record: see guide 3 · Zoom → Recording.

🚧 Boundaries with students

  • No private services. Do not offer or give private lessons or outside courses to any MLI student, or to their family members or friends.
  • No personal contacts. Do not add students on WeChat or WhatsApp personally. Use the class group chat on the platform.
  • Make-ups and cancellations go through the school. Do not arrange them directly with students. For private (1-on-1) classes, ask the student to inform Academic Affairs.
  • Administrative questions (fees, registration, schedule changes) go to the administration or the academic coordinator.
  • Confidentiality. Student information and school documents stay inside MLI.
